Dragon Village
--------------
DragonVillageEntry:
	areas: vanilla spawn, ember pre-bridge, elder pre-dj
	connected to DragonVillageDJ (requires DJ)
	connected to DragonVillageToCroc (requires DJ)
	connected to DragonVillageGnastyCave (requires boss lair rule)

DragonVillageDepotShop
	shop region
	connected to DragonVillageEntry (requires shop enabled)

DragonVillageDJ:
	areas: double jump ledge and lower byrd area, hunter until end of middle cave (ends with charge blocker)
	connected to DragonVillageEntry (requires nothing)
	connected to DragonVillageGlide (requires glide)
	connected to DragonVillageCharge (requires charge)

DragonVillageToCroc:
	areas: ember post-bridge
	connected to CrocovileEntry (requires nothing)

DragonVillageGlide:
	areas: hunter after cave until his gate (ends with hunter gate blocker if coming backwards)

DragonVillageBallGadget:
	areas: post-hunter and nursery (ends with ball gadget blocker)

DragonVillageCharge:
	areas: upper byrd area and lab path (ends with no blocker)

DragonVillageGnastyCave:
	areas: gnasty cave
	connected to DragonVillageEntry (requires nothing)

Crocovile Swamp
---------------
CrocovileEntry:
	areas: after moving platform and pyramid area
		has Perilous Pyramid shop
	connected to DragonVillageToCroc (requires double jump)
	connected to CrocovileWestOfPyramid (requires double jump)

CrocovilePyramidShop
	shop region
	connected to CrocovileEntry (requires shop enabled)

CrocovileWestofPyramid
	areas: west of pyramid stuff above dark gem
	connected to nothing

CrocovileOutsideTemple
	areas: outside of temple AND tunnel leading up to the chargeable wall (but not past the drop-off)
		has Forgotten Temple shop
	connected to CrocovileInsideTemple (requires double jump)
	connected to CrocovileBlink1 (requires nothing)

CrocovileTempleShop
	shop region
	connected to CrocovileOutsideTemple (requires shop enabled)

CrocovileInsideTemple
	areas: inside temple
	connected to nothing

CrocovileEast
	areas: after the drop-off next to the chargeable wall, up through to the first set of poles
	connected to CrocovileOutsideTemple (requires double jump)
	connected to CrocovileOutsideTree (requires double jump)
	connected to CrocovilePoles1 (requires pole spin)

CrocovilePoles1
	areas: cave above first set of poles
	connected to CrocovileEast (requires nothing)
	connected to CrocovileOutsideTree (requires nothing)

CrocovileOutsideTree
	areas: near blink and outside elder magnus tree
		has Elder's Tree shop
	connected to CrocovileEast (requires double jump)
	connected to CrocovilePoles2 (requires pole spin)
	connected to CrocovileFredneck (requires pole spin)
	connected to CrocovileInsideTree1 (requires double jump)

CrocovileElderTreeShop
	shop region
	connected to CrocovileOutsideTree (requires shop enabled)

CrocovileInsideTree1
	areas: inside tree on ground (just the Magnus check)
	connected to CrocovileInsideTree2 (requires pole spin)

CrocovileInsideTree2
	areas: everything above pole spins in Magnus tree
	connected to nothing

CrocovilePoles2
	areas: poles outside elder tree
	connected to nothing

CrocovilePoles3
	areas: poles above blink leading to dark gem
	connected to CrocovileFredneck (requires double jump)

CrocovileFredneck
	areas: fredneck and the lily pads back to pyramid
	connected to CrocovileEntry (requires glide)
