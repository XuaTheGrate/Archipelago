Dragon Village
--------------
DVEntry: shop, ember, and elder areas
	needs realm access card coming from CREntry, FVEntry, or SBEntry
	free entry if open world mode (teleport to shop)
DVGnastyCave: gnasty cave
	needs boss lair rule always (only can come from DVEntry)
DVByrdLower: lower part of byrd area
	needs double jump always (only can come from DVEntry)
DVByrdUpper: upper part of byrd area
	needs charge always (only can come from DVByrdLower)
DVLabSecret: path to lab secret entrance
	needs charge always (only can come from DVByrdLower)
DVHunterPreGlide: hunter section before glide
	needs double jump always (only can come from DVEntry)
DVHunterPostGlide: hunter section after glide
	needs glide always (only can come from DVHunterPreGlide)
DVBallGadget: ball gadget area
	free entry from DVHunterPostGlide
	needs ball gadget if open world mode
DVTunnelToCroc: across bridge by ember
	needs double jump always (can come from either DVEntry or CSPyramid)

Crocovile Swamp
---------------
CSPyramid: vanilla entryway and pyramid
	needs double jump if coming from DVEntry
	needs glide if coming from CSFredneck
	free entry if open world mode (teleport to shop)
CSWestOfPyramid: west area of map
	needs double jump always (only can come from CSPyramid)
CSTemple: forgotten temple area, including tunnel after with drop-off
	free entry from CSPyramid
	needs double jump coming from CSEast
	free entry if open world mode (teleport to shop)
CSEast: vanilla first half of area with Blink
	free entry from CSTemple
	needs double jump coming from CSOutsideTree
CSEastPoles1: above first set of poles. Only has 1 enemy
	needs pole spin coming from CSEast
CSEastPoles2: rest of cave above first set of poles
	needs double jump coming from CSEastPoles1
CSOutsideTree: outside elder magnus tree and near Blink
	needs double jump coming from CSEast
	free entry if open world (teleport to shop)
CSOutsideTreePoles: above poles directly across tree (not path to Fredneck)
	needs pole spin coming from CSOutsideTree
CSInsideTreeLower: inside elder magnus tree
	need double jump coming from CSOutsideTree
CSInsideTreeUpper: going up poles in tree
	need pole spin coming from CSInsideTreeLower
CSPathToFredneck: cave past poles near Blink
	need pole spin coming from CSOutsideTree
CSFredneck: Fredneck area itself and going back to pyramid
	need double jump coming from CSPathToFredneck

Dragonfly Falls
---------------
DFBallPath: ball gadget path
	needs ball gadget if coming from DVBallGadget or DFEntry
DFEntry: entryway post-ball gadget
	free entry from DFBallPath
